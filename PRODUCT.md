# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want a practical daily calorie and macronutrient tracker, especially
users in the Philippines who need familiar local dishes and grocery products
alongside international food databases. They use TrackBing in short,
task-focused sessions to find or scan food, review serving nutrition, log it,
and understand how the day compares with a personalized target.

## Product Purpose

TrackBing makes food logging fast and understandable across mobile and web. It
combines barcode lookup, personal foods and recipes, Filipino food coverage,
serving-unit conversion, daily macro totals, and a profile-based nutrition
target. Success means users can record food with minimal friction, trust where
an estimate came from, and continue consistently without shame-based coaching
or misleading health promises.

## Brand Personality

Supportive, grounded, and quietly capable. The product should feel warm enough
to encourage repeat use, restrained enough for dense nutrition data, and
transparent about calculated or AI-estimated values. Bee adds personality
through concise situational guidance without turning the app into a toy.

## Anti-references

TrackBing should not resemble a neon gym dashboard, a generic gradient-heavy AI
app, a glassmorphic card collection, or a childish mascot game. It should also
avoid punitive diet language, medical certainty, copied character designs,
oversized decorative cards, glow-heavy statistics, and motion that interrupts
logging.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next logging action within easy reach and keep common flows short.
2. Show users what is calculated, estimated, scanned, or personally saved.
3. Use Bee to explain state and encourage progress, never to shame or distract.
4. Preserve familiar controls and consistent behavior across phone, tablet, and
   desktop layouts.
5. Treat nutrition guidance as a transparent starting estimate, not a promise
   or diagnosis.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA across web and equivalent accessible behavior on native
platforms. Maintain readable contrast, labeled controls, keyboard access on
web, 44-by-44 logical-pixel touch targets, and text alongside color or pose
changes. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Coaching copy must avoid guilt,
shame, stigmatizing language, and unsupported medical claims.
